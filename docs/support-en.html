Frequently asked questions

How do I get started?

Sign in with Apple, then create a new Space or join one with an invitation code. You can also keep records in a Space you create just for yourself.

How do I store photos or videos?

Select photos or videos using “Add Record” in the timeline. You can also send supported photo and video files through “Save to INHWA” in the iOS share sheet. After sharing, open the INHWA app to check that the upload has finished. It is best not to delete the original files before the upload is complete.

My upload failed.

Check your internet connection and try again in the INHWA app. If you need to import the files again, select the originals in the Photos app. INHWA cannot recover an original download link from a photo studio if the link has expired or the provider has restricted access.

I denied QR scanning or location permission.

You can enter the six-character invitation code manually. You can search for a photo location without location permission, or select it directly on the map and enter a name. You can change permissions in iPhone Settings.

Who can see my records?

Members of the same Space can view and download records. New members can also see existing records, so share invitation QR codes and codes only with the people you want to join.

Is leaving a Space different from deleting my account?

Yes. Records you uploaded remain in the Space after you leave it. Signing out does not delete server data either. Account deletion deletes your account and the data you uploaded. Records uploaded by other members are retained.

Account deletion

Start at Spaces tab → Settings (gear icon) at the top right → Account Settings → Delete Account. Read the deletion notice and follow the on-screen steps, including Apple reauthentication.

If deletion is interrupted, resume it in the app. If you cannot access the sign-in or deletion screen, contact scijerry.official@gmail.com. We will provide guidance after checking the minimum information needed to verify your identity.

Once account deletion is complete, the account and data cannot be recovered. INHWA cannot remotely delete copies that other members have already downloaded to the Photos app or elsewhere. See the Privacy Policy for the detailed scope and retention criteria.

Reporting content or rights violations

If you suspect illegal content, photo sharing without consent, harassment, or infringement of copyright or portrait rights, email scijerry.official@gmail.com with [INHWA Report] in the subject line.

  • Information that identifies the Space and record concerned
  • The time of the incident, the reason for the report and the rights infringed
  • An email address where you can be reached and the minimum necessary supporting evidence

Do not attach illegally recorded material or child sexual abuse material itself. In the app, submitting a report through another member’s record → More → Report hides that record from your view. Blocking through the member menu restricts both users from viewing each other’s records and profiles across all Spaces. An email report does not automatically hide content or block users. After review, we will explain any necessary action and additional information needed. If you disagree with an action, you can request a review at the same email address. Report immediate danger to the police or other relevant authorities as well.

Privacy requests

Send requests for access, correction, deletion, suspension of processing or withdrawal of consent to scijerry.official@gmail.com. To prevent disclosure of another person’s information, we may verify that you are the person concerned or their legal representative.
