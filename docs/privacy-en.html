Privacy Policy

Effective date · Last updated: October 7, 2026 / Version 1.1

SCIJERRY (the “Operator”) processes personal information as described below to provide INHWA’s account, photo and video storage, and invitation-based Space services. This policy applies to the INHWA app and this information website.

1. Information processed and purposes

Accounts and sign-in

We use the user identifier, name and email address provided through Sign in with Apple (an Apple relay email address if you choose Hide My Email), along with your INHWA account identifier and authentication information, to create and authenticate your account and verify access permissions. INHWA does not collect your Apple Account password.

Records you select or enter

We use photos, videos, capture dates, notes, photo location names, addresses and coordinates, tags for people you were with, Space names, profile photos and membership relationships to store, retrieve, share and export records. Original photos and videos may contain metadata such as capture dates and locations. Tags for people you were with refer to Space membership relationships; this feature does not read your iPhone address book.

Permissions and optional features

  • Importing photos and videos: We use the items you select in the system picker or explicitly send through the share sheet.
  • Adding to the Photos app: We use save permission when you request an export.
  • Camera: Used to recognize invitation QR codes. This feature does not upload the camera video itself to the server.
  • Location: If permitted, used to search for nearby places and display distances. The coordinates of a saved photo location are stored on the server as part of the record. We do not continuously track your movements.

If you deny an optional permission, you can still use features that do not require it. You can enter invitation codes manually and specify places manually.

Report and block information

For in-app reports, we use the identifiers of the reporter, reported user, record and Space, the report reason and description, and the submission time for Operator review and safety measures. When you block someone, we store the blocking relationship between users to restrict access to records and profiles and create a submission for Operator review. The Operator is automatically notified by email when a report or block is submitted.

Connection and inquiry information

When you connect to the service, infrastructure providers may process your IP address, request time, errors, and authentication and security logs. This is for providing connections, responding to service disruptions and preventing misuse. For inquiries and reports, we use your email address, message and any supporting evidence you send to respond, resolve problems and protect rights.

We do not use advertising tracking, cross-app tracking, sales of personal information for advertising, or separate SDKs for analyzing usage behavior. Some diagnostic values, such as the number of place search results in the app, are stored only on the device.

2. Sharing within Spaces

Members of the same Space can view and download records and media. Members can see information such as display names, roles and the people tagged as being together. Newly invited members can also see existing records.

Invitation QR codes and codes grant access to join a Space, so do not post them publicly. Sharing is not through a public feed, but INHWA cannot control copies that another person has already saved or shared elsewhere.

The Operator may access data as necessary to operate the service, respond to service disruptions and handle reports. We do not otherwise disclose it at our discretion except in response to a request under applicable law, with separate consent, or to provide the services described in this policy.

3. External services and entrusted processing

  • Supabase: Provides account authentication, the database, private media storage and server functions. The app’s primary storage region is Seoul, South Korea. Account information, records and media, membership relationships, and service request information are processed. Data Processing Addendum · Subprocessor list
  • Apple: We use system services for Sign in with Apple, maps and place searches, photo selection and export, and QR recognition. The Apple Privacy Policy also applies to information processed by each service.
  • Resend: Sends report and block submission notifications to the Operator’s email address. It processes the submission identifier, type and time, and the Operator’s recipient address. Photos, videos, report descriptions and user names are not included in notification emails. Resend Privacy Policy
  • Google Gmail: When you send an inquiry or report by email, the email service processes your email address, message and attachments. Google Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Pages: Hosts this public information website. GitHub may process visitors’ IP addresses and connection information for security and other purposes. This website has no separate advertising or analytics scripts or account registration feature. GitHub Privacy Statement

When you search for a place, your search term and approximate location (in units of about 1 km) are sent to Kakao (KakaoMap Local API) to receive search results, and INHWA does not store them.

A storage region in Seoul does not mean that all processing occurs only within Korea. Processing may occur outside Korea through overseas providers’ authentication, network, email and support services and their subprocessors. The processing locations and retention criteria of those services are described in the provider documents above. You can request details of the processing that applies to INHWA using the contact information below.

When you import from a web link, the service connects to the website you shared to find media. That website may process connection information such as your IP address, and its own policies also apply. INHWA does not remove third-party websites’ access restrictions or grant download rights.

4. Retention periods and deletion

  • Accounts and records: Retained for as long as needed to provide the service, and deleted from the operational database and storage when deletion of the account or relevant data is completed.
  • Signing out: Ends the device’s signed-in session but does not delete your account or records from the server.
  • Leaving a Space: Ends your membership, but records you uploaded to that Space remain.
  • Deleting a Space: When the owner deletes a Space, all records and media uploaded by its members, invitations and membership information in that Space are deleted.
  • Account deletion: Deletes your account and the data you uploaded. Records uploaded by other members are retained. Spaces with remaining members are retained by transferring ownership, and Spaces with no remaining members are removed.
  • Deletion processing records: The minimum information needed to resume an interrupted deletion is retained until completion. Account deletion completion receipts are disassociated from the user identifier and become eligible for cleanup 30 days after the app acknowledges receipt of completion. Before acknowledgment, they are retained so that the deletion completion status can be recovered. The minimum identifiers and processing status needed to prevent data from being uploaded again to deleted Spaces are retained on an ongoing basis; their association with the requester is removed when the requester’s account is deleted.
  • Device copies: Files waiting to be uploaded and viewing caches may be stored on the device. Local data for the account is cleared during account deletion. Files exported to the Photos app and copies held by other people must be deleted separately.
  • Inquiries and reports: Retained for as long as needed to handle them and resolve related disputes, then deleted without delay when the purpose has been fulfilled. If retention is required by law, the relevant items are stored separately for the required period.

Electronic information is destroyed through the service’s database and storage deletion procedures. Infrastructure security logs and backup copies may be removed under the provider’s retention and rotation policies, so deletion of operational data and deletion of all infrastructure copies may not occur at the same time. If deletion is interrupted by a server failure or another issue, use the app’s resume feature or contact support.

5. Your rights and how to exercise them

You can request access to personal information, correction or deletion, suspension of processing, or withdrawal of consent at scijerry.official@gmail.com. We may request the minimum information needed to verify that you are the person concerned or their legal representative. If a request is restricted by law, we will explain the reason.

Start account deletion at Spaces → Settings (gear icon) → Account Settings → Delete Account. You can withdraw system permissions in iPhone Settings. If you do not agree to essential account processing or stop that processing, sign-in-based storage and sharing services may be restricted.

We protect minors’ personal information under the same standards. If you become aware that personal information requiring a legal representative’s consent has been processed without consent, a guardian or user should notify us using the contact information above. After verification, we will explain the necessary protection and deletion measures.

6. Protection measures

We use HTTPS for server connections and restrict data access based on sign-in and Space membership. Media storage is private and uses expiring access links. This does not mean that the service provides end-to-end encryption. Take care not to expose invitation codes or access links to other people.
