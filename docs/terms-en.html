Terms of Use & Content Rules

Effective date · Last updated: October 7, 2026 / Version 1.1

These terms explain the basic conditions for using INHWA, provided by SCIJERRY (the “Operator”).

1. Service and accounts

INHWA lets you store and revisit photos and videos you select, along with capture dates, notes, locations and information about the people you were with, on your own or in invitation-based Spaces. Accounts use Sign in with Apple. Please keep your account and device secure.

You must not use another person’s account or distribute invitation codes without authorization. Some features may be unavailable due to disruptions or changes to external services, network conditions or other factors.

2. Rights to photos and videos

Rights to content remain with the original rights holder. You must upload only material you own or have permission to store and share, and respect the privacy and portrait rights of people shown in it and the provider’s terms of use.

You authorize the Operator to store, process and transmit material only as needed to provide the storage, Space sharing and export features you select in INHWA. This authorization does not transfer ownership of the content or grant the right to use it for advertising.

INHWA does not claim to be an official partner service of any particular photo studio or brand. Sharing a web link alone does not grant download rights, and you must not bypass access restrictions or paid-use conditions. We do not guarantee recovery of expired original download links or ongoing compatibility with third-party websites.

3. Sharing and deletion in Spaces

Members can view and download records in the same Space, and newly joined members can access existing records. Choose carefully whom you invite.

Leaving a Space or signing out does not automatically delete records you uploaded. When the owner deletes a Space, all records in that Space are deleted. Account deletion deletes your account and the data you uploaded; other members’ records are retained. Exported copies must be managed separately. See the retention and deletion information for details.

4. Prohibited content and conduct

  • Illegally recorded material, private images shared without consent, sexually explicit content, child or adolescent sexual exploitation material, and other illegal content
  • Threats, harassment, promotion of hatred or violence, and unauthorized disclosure of another person’s identifying information
  • Material that infringes another person’s copyright, portrait rights, privacy or other rights
  • Malicious files, spam, impersonation, unauthorized access, or circumvention of security or access restrictions
  • Conduct that interferes with normal service operation or harms other users

5. Reports and responses

Report suspected violations to scijerry.official@gmail.com with [INHWA Report] in the subject line. Identify the material and reason for the report and provide the minimum necessary supporting evidence. After review, we will explain any necessary action. Do not attach illegal material itself or account passwords.

For confirmed violations, the Operator may take necessary action, including removing content, in accordance with applicable law and the facts. You can request an explanation or review of an action at the same email address. In the app, you can submit a report with a reason through another member’s record → More → Report. The reported record is hidden from the reporter’s view. Blocking through the member menu restricts both users from viewing each other’s records and profiles across all Spaces. Report and block submissions are automatically brought to the Operator’s attention for review; blocking is different from removing a member from a Space, and copies already downloaded cannot be retrieved. Email reports alone do not activate hiding or blocking in the app.

The Operator may modify or temporarily suspend features for maintenance, security responses or changes to external services. Changes that materially affect use will be announced in advance where possible. Keep separate copies of important originals.

These terms do not limit users’ rights guaranteed by applicable law or exclude the Operator’s liability for intentional misconduct or gross negligence. Important changes to these terms will be announced before they take effect, and separate consent will be obtained where required.
